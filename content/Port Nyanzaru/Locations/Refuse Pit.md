# Refuse Pit

*Old City, Port Nyanzaru*

- An enormous sinkhole that serves as the city's dump
- Garbage, rancid water, rats, and ghouls
- The level of the pit rises and falls but doesn't overflow — due to the otyughs that live at the bottom
