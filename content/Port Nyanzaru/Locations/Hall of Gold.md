# Hall of Gold

*Market Ward, Port Nyanzaru*

- Sits on Mount Sibasa, the highest point in the city
- A temple to Waukeen
- Called the Hall of Gold because of its shining golden roof and Waukeen's focus on trade and wealth
- Some think the roof is pure gold
- A wide stone bridge connects it to the Grand Coliseum
- Mother Sibonseni
