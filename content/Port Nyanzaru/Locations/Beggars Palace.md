# Beggars Palace

*Old City, Port Nyanzaru*

- Three vine-covered ancient ziggurats
- Two connected by a bridge at the top
- Dozens of stories high
- Shops jammed into the terraces of the ziggurats
- Upper levels are less crowded
- Run by the Beggar Prince
