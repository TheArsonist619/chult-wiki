# Temple of Sune

*Merchant Ward, Port Nyanzaru*

- Atop a rocky formation in the sea
- Steep steps wind up from a small harbor
- When seen from a distance the temple seems to float (mirrors line the outside walls)
- Faithful and visitors enjoy the bathhouse, lessons in makeup and hair, and learning to dress for your profession
