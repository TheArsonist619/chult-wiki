# Public Bathouse

*Market Ward, Port Nyanzaru*

- A clean city, thanks to rain and residents who are big on hygiene
- Most are repulsed by the thought of going a day without a bath
- Dozens of small private bathhouses around the city
- Open to everyone here, run by priests of Sune
- No charge, but a donation is requested — even the poorest leave a copper piece
- Masseuses on hand for a cost
