## Session 1 - The Easten Eagle
You all meet on the Eastern Eagle, a ship en route to Port Nyanzaru, on the continent of Chult. After leaving the stormy waters everyone is on the deck when Sahaugin attack. The next night [[Ademar Beylinn]] is murdered by [[Laurel Magwin]] as she reveals her true Sea Hag form. [[Kael]] 

## Session 2- Port Nyanzaru
The party arrives in [[Port Nyanzaru]] and is greeted by [[Arameg]] the Dragon Turtle, who demands money from all who enter or leave the Port.
