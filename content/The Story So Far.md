## Session 1 - The Easten Eagle
You all meet on the Eastern Eagle, a ship en route to Port Nyanzaru, on the continent of Chult. After leaving the stormy waters everyone is on the deck when Sahaugin attack. [[Kael]] reveals himself as a stow-away and helps in the fight. Aftwards, [[Harindel Adna]] threatens to apprehend him upon arrival to [Port Nyanzaru]] The next night [[Ademar Beylinn]] is murdered by [[Laurel Magwin]] as she reveals her true Sea Hag form.

## Session 2- Port Nyanzaru
The party arrives in The Bay of Chult and is greeted by [[Arameg]] the Dragon Turtle, who demands money from all who enter or leave the Port.

The party finally arrive to Port Nyanzaru where they are greeted by a dinosaur race which they use to escape [[Harindel Adna]], with some help from [[Zemo Dizzywidget]]. [[Kael]] wins the race. [[Eman Wori]] tries to go straight to the jungle. 

## Session 3- Kael poops his pants
While [[Kael]] spends a few days adjusting to the local cuisine, the rest of the part explores a bit of [[Port Nyanzaru]], visiting [[Grandfather Zitembe]] at the Temple of Savras, assisting [[Ovak]] in clearing the [[Emerald Enclave]] base, and assisting [[Zemo Dizzywidget]] in clearing his old house in Malar's Throat. Afterwards they decide on [[Azaka Stormfang]] as their guide and begin planning their expedition into the jungle. 

## Session 4- Into The Jungle 
The venture into the jungle begins. Several fights are head over the course of a few days as they get their footing. They then encounter Ubtoa's Mane, a mushroom that all but [[Kael]] eat.

#### Mushroom Vision 
- For all who ingest, it is a 2 hour long journey across the universe. It's like being guided through your own life and future by a sentient hug. The warmest, safest hug you've ever had, that shows you terrifying and beautiful things.
- Constant waves of euphoria course through your body like a slow reverberating drum beat.
- Slowly towards the end the hug feels less like its embracing you and more like it's pulling you along more violently.
    - You see flashes of rot and disease spreading throughout the jungle at a rapid pace and then beyond, through the sea. All throughout the world you see different flashes of people all over the world, in complete despair. Some holding onto dead loved ones of all ages, some succumbing to wounds long since healed.
    - Then back to the jungle, you see hoards of undead and other monstrosities pour over Port Nyanzaru and then the Sword Coast and beyond. Through every flash and vision you feel the emotional weight of what is happening, both on an individual level and as whole, as the world heaves in turmoil.
    - Then the flashes stop, the despair lifts and suddenly you are flying under the jungle canopy, through rivers, lakes, mountains, at a speed you can barely comprehend.
    - Then you see it. A city. A massive city that rivals Port Nyanzaru. Surrounded by sheer cliffs on all sides. It appears to have once been a beautiful metropolis, now just a rundown shell. You begin to wonder the origins of this place and its location when suddenly the despair hits you again. As you get closer to the city, the feeling of agony becomes nearly unbearable, you feel as if you're body is being ripped apart. Then you wake up.
    - **Pip**: Right before you wake up, you see it again. A mask made of gnarled roots with various mushrooms growing all over. Zoom out and you see a dark cave, zoom out again and you see the cave opening is in the middle of a sheer cliff with a roaring river 100's of feet below. You know these aren't the same sheer cliffs you saw in the city because there was no river
