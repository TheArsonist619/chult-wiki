# Stinger

*Weapon (dagger), major, rare*

![[Images/Stinger.jpg|300]]

- **Damage**: 1d4 piercing
- **Range**: 20/60
- **Properties**: Finesse, Light, Thrown
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

You can use an action to cause thick, black poison to coat the blade. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 2d10 poison damage and become poisoned for 1 minute. The dagger can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

*Reskin of the Dagger of Venom (Dungeon Master's Guide p. 161, SRD)*
