# Emerald Enclave

The Emerald Enclave is a widespread order of wilderness survivalists devoted to preserving the natural order and rooting out unnatural threats. In Chult, they are chiefly concerned with protecting the living from the undead menace spread by the death curse. Only a handful of guides in Port Nyanzaru and Fort Beluarian are openly tied to the enclave, while others remain secret members or quiet sympathizers. The faction maintains several well-camouflaged outposts throughout the jungle that serve as hidden observation posts and a potential rescue for adventurers who get into trouble in the wild.
