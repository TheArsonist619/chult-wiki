# Flaming Fist

The Flaming Fist is a mercenary company contracted to help keep order in Port Nyanzaru and along the roads of Chult. Flaming Fist soldiers are easy to spot in their red-and-black uniforms, and patrols are a common sight around the city's gates, markets, and docks. They answer to their own command structure rather than the Merchant Princes, hired to supplement the city watch rather than replace it.
